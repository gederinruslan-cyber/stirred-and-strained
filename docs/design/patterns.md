# Design patterns

*Layouts, components, copy and states that features reuse. Filled by the `ui-ux-designer` pass
of each screen feature, starting with M0-06. Reuse before inventing; a new component or token
is a decision to call out.*

## Principles
- Desktop-first: filters beside results, My Bar beside "what can I make", side-by-side compare.
  Usable at 360 px without horizontal scroll.
- Trust is visible: attribution, finished ABV and standard drinks, AI labels.
- Zero-proof drinks are never visually second-class.
