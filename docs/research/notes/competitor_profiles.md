# Competitive Landscape: Cocktail Apps and Websites (as of Oct 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-31. App Store data fetched live; third-party "best apps" listicles are often vendor-written and contain contradictions (flagged below).

## App-by-app profiles (features, platforms, pricing, recipe counts, status)

### Takeaway
The market is split between (a) large editorial web databases (Difford's 20,000+, Make Me a Cocktail 3,500+, Kindred, Liquor.com, Punch), (b) polished Apple-only native apps (Mixel, Cocktail Party, Highball, Cocktail Flow), many of which are stale or barely maintained, and (c) a flood of small AI-native iOS apps from 2025-2026. Mixel is the most complete actively-maintained mobile app; Difford's is the deepest web database; Make Me a Cocktail is the most established web-first "what can I make" tool.

### Inferences
- The "classic" polished indie iOS apps (Highball 2018, Cocktail Flow 2019, Cocktail Party 2024) are aging or stagnant; Mixel is the main actively updated, well-rated premium native app. There is room for a modern, maintained product.
- No major player combines a large curated database (Difford's scale), excellent modern UX (Mixel/Highball scale), and a web-first experience. Difford's and Make Me a Cocktail are web-first but their UX looks dated; the nicest UX is Apple-only.
- AI features (photo bottle scanning, chat bartender, generate-from-inventory) became table stakes among 2025–2026 entrants, but these are mostly small apps with few ratings. Recipe accuracy and curation set apps apart (A Bar Above's test shows accuracy varies widely).

## Web-first vs mobile-only; "what can I make" strength

### Takeaway
Web-first: Difford's, Make Me a Cocktail, Kindred, Liquor.com, Punch, Bar Assistant/Salt Rim, Elixiary, BarGPT, Cocktail Party's catalog. Native-only (mostly Apple): Mixel, Highball, Cocktail Flow, Cocktail Party app, most AI entrants. Strongest "what can I make" tools: Make Me a Cocktail (with "What To Buy Next"), Cocktail Party (with substitutions and near-miss list), Mixel, Difford's Cabinet (paywalled), Bar Assistant (shopping lists from gaps).

### Cited Findings
- Make Me a Cocktail My Bar + What To Buy Next — [My Bar guide](https://makemeacocktail.com/myBar/guide)
- Cocktail Party check-off ingredients, substitutions; near-miss list noted by Redditors — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id928904094); [r/cocktails](https://lr.psf.lt/r/cocktails/comments/1k3b6k8/app_for_recipes_with_available_ingredients)
- Difford's cabinet search is Premium-only — [Difford's Premium](https://www.diffordsguide.com/premium)
- Mixel inventory matching (full library paywalled) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/US/app/id1280464759)
- Highball has no inventory matching — [Home Bar Hero](https://homebarhero.ai/blog/best-free-cocktail-apps/)
- Home Bar Hero frames inventory matching as the core value most users need — [Home Bar Hero](https://homebarhero.ai/blog/best-free-cocktail-apps/)

### Inferences
- A free, excellent, web-based "what can I make + what to buy next + substitutions" feature would match the best paid features (Difford's Premium, Mixel Premium).

## Monetization models and price points

### Takeaway
Models in use: one-time purchase ($3.99 Cocktail Party; Mixel $14.99/$49.99 lifetime), subscriptions (Difford's ~£50/yr; Cocktail Flow £4.99/mo or £24.99/yr; Home Bar Hero $2.99/mo), tip jar (Highball), ads + sponsorship (Kindred, Difford's free tier, editorial sites), seasonal content packs ($0.99), hardware (Barsys $280–375), physical subscription boxes (Shaker & Spoon), and affiliates (Barsys on Awin).

### Cited Findings
- See per-app prices above — [Mixel](https://apps.apple.com/US/app/id1280464759); [Cocktail Party](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id928904094); [Cocktail Flow](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id486811622); [Difford's](https://www.diffordsguide.com/premium); [Highball](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/highball-by-studio-neat/id973319934); [Kindred](https://kindredcocktails.com/info/faq); [Barsys](https://360.barsys.com/); [Home Bar Hero](https://homebarhero.ai/blog/best-free-cocktail-apps/)
- AI usage is the typical paywall lever (Mixel: 5 free BART chats/month; Bar Maestro: camera inventory Premium-only) — [Mixel](https://apps.apple.com/US/app/id1280464759); [Bar Maestro](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6757890867)

### Inferences
- Common price points: $3–5/mo, $15–30/yr, $4–15 one-time; lifetime ~$50. Users praise "no ads, no subscription" positioning (Cocktail Party, Barsys "no subscription, ever").

### Gaps
- No revenue figures for any cocktail app; no evidence found of spirits-purchase affiliate links inside apps (beyond Barsys affiliate program and Vivino's wine shop).

## Reviews and shutdowns

### Takeaway
Best-rated: Mixel 4.8 (1.5K), Cocktail Flow 4.6 (2.6K, UK), Cocktail Party 4.5 (218), Highball 4.4 (283). Praise centers on design and curation; complaints concern missing sync/backup and stale apps. No confirmed shutdowns found among the named apps; the main pattern is "abandonware" (still listed, no updates).

### Cited Findings
- Ratings — App Store listings above.
- Highball users praise visuals, but say it hasn't been updated in years — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/highball-by-studio-neat/id973319934)
- Accuracy varies: 2015 test rated Cocktail Flow 12/12, Highball 9/12, Mixology 7/12 (cups-vs-ounces error), DrinksFree 5/12 — [A Bar Above](https://abarabove.com/cocktail-apps-review/)
- Shaker & Spoon portal temporarily closed for migration (not a shutdown) — [FAQ](https://shakerandspoon.com/pages/faqs)

### Inferences
- Users value trust (accurate, tested recipes) and longevity/sync. Abandoned apps show room for a well-maintained product.

### Gaps
- No press coverage of notable cocktail app shutdowns found in 2024–2026.

## AI-native cocktail apps (2024–2026)

### Takeaway
There are many AI-native entrants, almost all small iOS apps: Home Bar Hero, Bar Maestro, BARAI, Sip AI, Spritz, OpenBar, Elixirate, BarShelf, BarGPT, Elixiary. Common features: photo/label bottle scanning to build inventory, chat "AI bartender," and generating recipes from what's on hand. Mixel is the established app that added AI (BART).

### Cited Findings
- See "Other notable entrants" above with sources.

### Inferences
- AI generation alone isn't enough to stand out; pairing AI with a trusted, curated database and accurate specs is the likely edge.
